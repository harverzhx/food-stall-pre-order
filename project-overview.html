Core Functionalities
  • Customer App: Browse menu, pre-order, schedule pickup, and pay via cash or e-wallet.
  • Vendor Dashboard: Real-time order notifications, menu management, and inventory control.
  • Order Status Tracking: Pending → Preparing → Ready for Pickup.
  • Flexible Payment Options: Cash on Pickup and E-wallet integration.
  • Multi-Stall Support: Scalable system for multiple campus food vendors.
  • Basic Sales Reports: Daily and weekly performance analytics for vendors.

Limitations

A transparent outline of the technical and functional boundaries of the current project phase.

Technical Boundaries
  • Pickup-Only Model: No delivery services or courier logistics.
  • No AI Integration: No automated recommendations or chatbot features.
  • External Accounting: No integration with external accounting or inventory software.
  • Offline Mode: Limited functionality when internet connectivity is lost.
  • Single-Platform: No cross-platform compatibility for all devices.

Project Objectives

Our goals are focused on streamlining campus food services through technology, ensuring a seamless experience for both students and vendors.

  • Reduce waiting time and long queues during peak hours (recess, lunch).
  • Easy vendor tool for menus, orders, inventory, and sales reports.
  • Mobile-friendly pre-ordering with flexible pickup scheduling.
  • Multiple payment options including cash on pickup and e-wallets.
  • Improve customer satisfaction and stall efficiency through real-time tracking.
  • Multi-stall support and real-time order notifications for vendors.
